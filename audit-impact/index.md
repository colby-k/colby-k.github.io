<section id="cases" class="sp-section sp-section--paper">
  <div class="sp-heading">
    <div><p class="sp-kicker sp-kicker--dark">Case study 01</p><h2>Building and strengthening an Internal Audit function.</h2></div>
    <p>Academy Mortgage · Senior Internal Audit Manager</p>
  </div>
  <div class="sp-list-grid">
    <article class="sp-list-card"><span>Challenge</span><h3>Create a consistent operating model.</h3><p>Establish an Internal Audit function capable of executing risk-based financial, operational, compliance, and ITGC work in a regulated mortgage environment.</p></article>
    <article class="sp-list-card"><span>What I did</span><h3>Built the structure for the function.</h3><p>Established risk-based planning, testing methodologies, reporting protocols, team-development practices, and stronger review processes.</p></article>
    <article class="sp-list-card"><span>Result</span><h3>45% less report rework.</h3><p>Built and coached the audit team while reducing report rework through standardized training, clearer quality expectations, and stronger review discipline.</p></article>
    <article class="sp-list-card"><span>Leadership value</span><h3>Quality became part of the operating model.</h3><p>The improvement was not a one-time edit to reports; it came from creating repeatable expectations for how work was prepared, reviewed, and communicated.</p></article>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="sp-section">
  <div class="sp-heading">
    <div><p class="sp-kicker sp-kicker--dark">Case study 02</p><h2>Expanding audit coverage with full-population testing.</h2></div>
    <p>Academy Mortgage · SQL and Python</p>
  </div>
  <div class="sp-list-grid">
    <article class="sp-list-card"><span>Challenge</span><h3>Large populations limited visibility.</h3><p>Traditional sampling alone could not provide the same view of patterns, concentrations, timing, and exceptions across large transaction populations.</p></article>
    <article class="sp-list-card"><span>What I did</span><h3>Automated testing across 50K+ transactions.</h3><p>Used SQL and Python to apply defined testing logic across the full population and direct detailed audit attention toward higher-risk records and exceptions.</p></article>
    <article class="sp-list-card"><span>Result</span><h3>Broader coverage and more efficient testing.</h3><p>Expanded the population visible to the audit team while improving the efficiency of exception identification and detailed follow-up.</p></article>
    <article class="sp-list-card"><span>Leadership value</span><h3>Analytics changed where audit effort went.</h3><p>The technology supported the audit decision by improving selection and focus; evidence evaluation and the final conclusion remained with the auditor.</p></article>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="sp-section sp-section--dark">

    <p>Alsco · Internal Audit Manager</p>
  </div>
  <div class="sp-list-grid">
    <article class="sp-list-card"><span>Scope</span><h3>Five international divisions.</h3><p>Led financial and operational audit initiatives in a multinational environment where consistent execution and risk visibility mattered across divisions.</p></article>
    <article class="sp-list-card"><span>People</span><h3>Six auditors managed and coached.</h3><p>Developed the team around audit quality, execution consistency, delivery standards, and practical use of analytics and risk indicators.</p></article>
    <article class="sp-list-card"><span>What I did</span><h3>Automated alerts and audit thresholds.</h3><p>Used analytics, dashboards, key risk indicators, automated alerts, and thresholds to focus audit attention and reduce recurring manual testing.</p></article>
    <article class="sp-list-card"><span>Result</span><h3>120+ hours reduced annually.</h3><p>Reduced manual testing effort by more than 120 hours per year while strengthening visibility into emerging financial and operational risks.</p></article>

    <div><p class="sp-kicker sp-kicker--dark">Case study 04</p><h2>Improving risk visibility through business intelligence.</h2></div>
    <p>Mountain America Credit Union · Internal Audit, Compliance &amp; Legal Analytics</p>
  </div>
  <div class="sp-list-grid">
    <article class="sp-list-card"><span>Challenge</span><h3>Risk information needed to be easier to use.</h3><p>Internal Audit, Compliance, and Legal needed repeatable reporting and analytical views that translated business and testing needs into clearer risk information.</p></article>
    <article class="sp-list-card"><span>What I did</span><h3>Built dashboards and automated reporting.</h3><p>Translated risk and testing needs into SQL analyses, automated reporting, exception monitoring, and business-intelligence solutions.</p></article>
    <article class="sp-list-card"><span>Result</span><h3>10+ Power BI dashboards launched.</h3><p>Designed and launched more than 10 dashboards that improved reporting efficiency, issue visibility, and stakeholder access to key risk indicators.</p></article>
    <article class="sp-list-card"><span>Control discipline</span><h3>Validated outputs for audit readiness.</h3><p>Developed exception reports and validation routines to strengthen the accuracy, completeness, and reliability of analytical outputs.</p></article>
