<section class="sp-section sp-section--dark">
  <div class="sp-heading">
    <div><p class="sp-kicker">Responsible technology</p><h2>Use technology where it improves the audit decision.</h2></div>
    <p>The value is not the sophistication of the tool. It is whether the approach improves coverage, evidence quality, consistency, focus, execution, or review without weakening accountability.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="sp-list-grid">
    <article class="sp-list-card"><span>Analytics</span><h3>See more of the population.</h3><p>Use explainable tests, trends, relationships, and exception logic to decide where detailed procedures should concentrate.</p></article>
    <article class="sp-list-card"><span>Automation</span><h3>Remove avoidable manual work.</h3><p>Automate repeatable mechanics and monitoring steps while retaining review points for assumptions, exceptions, and conclusions.</p></article>
    <article class="sp-list-card"><span>AI</span><h3>Accelerate preparation and review.</h3><p>Use source-bound workflows for synthesis, documentation, comparison, and analysis while requiring human validation and approval.</p></article>
    <article class="sp-list-card"><span>Workflow design</span><h3>Fit the way auditors actually work.</h3><p>Adoption is part of the control environment. A technically strong solution that does not fit the workflow will not create sustainable improvement.</p></article>
  </div>
</section>
