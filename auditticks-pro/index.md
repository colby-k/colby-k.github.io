<section id="product" class="sp-statement">
  <p class="sp-kicker sp-kicker--dark">The product</p>
  <h2>Auditors should spend time evaluating evidence—not rebuilding the same documentation mechanics.</h2>
  <p>Tickmarks, legends, references, notes, callouts, formatting, and workpaper structures are small tasks individually. Repeated across samples and engagements, they create friction, inconsistency, and avoidable review effort. AuditTicks Pro places those actions in one purpose-built Excel ribbon while leaving the auditor responsible for the procedure, evidence, and conclusion.</p>
</section>

<section class="sp-section">
  <div class="sp-heading">
    <div><p class="sp-kicker sp-kicker--dark">Product scope</p><h2>One ribbon for recurring workpaper actions.</h2></div>
    <p>The product is organized around the mechanics auditors repeatedly perform before, during, and after testing. It supports documentation; it does not automate professional judgment.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="atp-feature-map">
    <article><span>01 · Mark</span><h3>In-cell and floating tickmarks</h3><p>Insert standardized audit symbols directly into cells or place movable marks over the worksheet, including Roman numerals, numbered and lettered sets, common symbols, and tie-out references.</p></article>
    <article><span>02 · Explain</span><h3>Annotations, notes, and callouts</h3><p>Add arrows, braces, rectangles, callouts, emphasis, and contextual notes so the workpaper is understandable without excessive narrative.</p></article>
    <article><span>03 · Standardize</span><h3>Legends and workpaper structures</h3><p>Generate consistent legends and begin with structured detailed-testing, document-review, or schedule formats rather than rebuilding the basic layout.</p></article>
    <article><span>04 · Refine</span><h3>Formatting and practical utilities</h3><p>Use font, fill, reference, and worksheet tools designed around common audit actions and the needs of both preparers and reviewers.</p></article>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="sp-section sp-section--dark" id="compatibility">
  <div class="sp-heading">
    <div><p class="sp-kicker">Compatibility and deployment</p><h2>Designed for controlled use in desktop Excel.</h2></div>
    <p>AuditTicks Pro is currently a VBA-based XLAM add-in for Microsoft Excel on Windows. Broader distribution requires an operating model—not only a working file.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="atp-compatibility">
    <article><span>Environment</span><h3>Excel desktop for Windows</h3><p>The current product is designed for the Windows desktop version of Excel. Support for Excel for the web or macOS is not currently represented.</p></article>
    <article><span>Permissions</span><h3>Add-in and macro controls apply</h3><p>Installation depends on the organization’s Excel add-in, VBA, trusted-location, digital-signing, and endpoint-management policies.</p></article>
    <article><span>Distribution</span><h3>Evaluation is controlled</h3><p>The add-in is not presented as a public download. Demonstration and early evaluation should use a known version, defined installation steps, and a controlled update path.</p></article>
  </div>
  <div class="atp-boundary"><strong>Deployment boundary</strong><p>Each organization remains responsible for reviewing the add-in, testing it in its environment, applying information-security and software-distribution requirements, and determining whether it is approved for use.</p></div>
</section>
