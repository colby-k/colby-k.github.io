<section class="sp-section">
  <div class="sp-heading">
    <div><p class="sp-kicker sp-kicker--dark">How I lead</p><h2>Four principles that shape the work.</h2></div>
    <p>The goal is sound judgment, clear evidence, and repeatable quality without unnecessary process.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="sp-list-grid">
    <article class="sp-list-card"><span>01 · Risk before procedure</span><h3>Start with the assurance need.</h3><p>Keep scope, testing, evidence, and conclusions connected to the risk the engagement is intended to address.</p></article>
    <article class="sp-list-card"><span>02 · Evidence before assertion</span><h3>Make conclusions traceable.</h3><p>Require relevant, sufficient support and a visible path from procedure to evidence to conclusion.</p></article>
    <article class="sp-list-card"><span>03 · Review develops judgment</span><h3>Improve the next decision.</h3><p>Use manager review to explain reasoning gaps so auditors can apply the lesson independently on future work.</p></article>
    <article class="sp-list-card"><span>04 · Improvement fits the work</span><h3>Modernize with purpose.</h3><p>Use methodology, analytics, automation, or AI where it materially improves coverage, quality, reviewability, or efficiency.</p></article>
  </div>
</section>
