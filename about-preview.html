The idea behind the work

Recurring audit friction is usually a systems problem.

When teams repeatedly rebuild documentation, reshape data, search for methodology, or correct inconsistent work, the answer is rarely to work harder. It is to design a clearer, more reusable way of working—while keeping professional judgment at the center.

Working principles

How I approach audit innovation.

The goal is not technology for its own sake. It is better work: more consistent, more traceable, easier to review, and focused on the risks that matter.

01 · Judgment first

Technology supports the decision.

Automation and AI should improve the work around professional judgment—not make the judgment.

02 · Practical before clever

Adoption is part of the design.

A solution creates value only when it fits the way auditors actually work.

03 · Consistency compounds

Standards improve every engagement.

Reusable documentation, data logic, and review patterns reduce interpretation and rework.

04 · Evidence stays visible

Clarity creates trust.

Reviewers should be able to trace an analysis, workflow, or conclusion back to its support.
